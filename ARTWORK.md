# Artwork notes

The decorative cover collage and the background removal for the Benin head were created with the built-in image generation tool. The supplied reference image was used for visual style only and is not redistributed in this repository.

## Cover prompt

Create a landscape 16:9 background for an elegant vintage academic museum presentation. Do not render text. Use muted dusty slate blue gray, ivory paper, subtle aged paper texture and an archival collage. Leave the left 57% as dusty blue mottled paper for large ivory typography. On the right, combine an antique Greco-Roman marble head and shoulder in profile facing left with a smaller bronze African royal head, faint nineteenth-century architectural engravings and torn cream paper. Objects are decorative artistic interpretations, not documentary reproductions of specific artifacts. Use subtle warm ivory marble, muted bronze, rough cutout edges, delicate photographic grain and soft faded shadows. No modern room, frame, captions or diagrams.

## Background removal prompt

Remove the gray studio background and cast shadow from the Cleveland Museum of Art's open-access photograph of the ancestral head, accession 1938.6. Preserve the bronze head's shape, crown, surface and colours. Use a transparent background and a small margin. Do not add elements or text.

Real case-study photographs and engravings are identified separately in the website's Sources dialog.
